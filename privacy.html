Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

AIOS Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool run by one independent creator (the owner). It connects only to social media accounts the owner controls, to upload the owner's own videos and read their performance. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): uploads videos to the owner's channels, and reads those channels' videos, view counts, comments and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • TikTok: posts videos to the owner's account and reads basic account and video information.
  • Instagram and Facebook: posts videos to the owner's account and Page and reads their insights.

How data is used and stored

  • Video statistics and the text of top comments are saved on the owner's own computer, only to decide which videos work better. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a private settings file.
  • No data from other people's accounts is collected. Public comments on the owner's videos are read as shown by the platform and can be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove this app's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

Removing the app's access on any platform stops all further access. The owner can delete the saved statistics file at any time; stored data is deleted within 30 days of a request.
