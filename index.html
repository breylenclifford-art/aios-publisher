AIOS Publisher

AIOS Publisher is a personal tool used by one independent creator to post their own original short videos to their own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, and to read how those videos perform.

It serves two of the creator's channels:

  • Story Shorts: short original fiction stories narrated over the creator's own gameplay footage (Minecraft and Roblox).
  • ASMR cooking: short cooking videos.

The videos are made with the help of AI tools. Every post is marked as AI-generated or altered content on each platform. The tool is not offered to the public and only connects to accounts its owner controls.
